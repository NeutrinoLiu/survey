## 1. Thesis — physical grounding must enter early

The diagnosis of why general VLMs fail as robot brains:
> *"existing VLMs still exhibit notable deficiencies in **capturing the granular details required for physical grounding**… Mainstream VLMs, **predominantly trained on static, web-scale datasets**, excel in general-purpose scenarios but remain inadequately optimized for embodied environments, **lacking the action-oriented capabilities essential for dynamic prediction, interaction, and planning**."*

The remedy is a *timing* claim about data:
> *"our training pipeline **embeds physical world understanding from the earliest phases of training**. Specifically, during the initial large-scale pre-training stage, we introduce a diverse and extensive corpus of visual perception data—spanning **2D and 3D grounding, depth estimation, and image segmentation**. This early integration fundamentally enhances the model's capacity to perceive and interpret complex physical environments."*

Same position as 1X's *"robotics is not a fine-tuning problem"* and PhysBrain's *"understanding first, action next"*, but with a specific prescription: **low-level geometric perception tasks (grounding, depth, segmentation) belong in the earliest pretraining mixture**, not in a later adaptation stage.

## 2. The action-centric capability taxonomy — curation driven by a spec

RxBrain 1.0's organizing contribution is that the data mixture is derived from a taxonomy rather than assembled opportunistically. Three progressive dimensions:

| Dimension | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Action-Relevant State Understanding** | What is the current physical state, in terms that bear on action? |
| **Action–Transition Reasoning** | How does state change under action? |
| **Sequential and Adaptive Reasoning** | Multi-step structure and adaptation |

> *"guided by this taxonomy, [Tencent] developed a **systematic data pipeline and curated data mixtures spanning both pre-training and post-training**."*

**Taxonomy → pipeline → mixture** is the reverse of the usual order (collect → filter → discover what you have). It makes coverage auditable: every capability in the taxonomy has an identifiable slice of the corpus, and gaps are visible by construction. Compare HumanNet's multi-axis taxonomy and Cosmos 3's 47 hierarchical categories — three independent works converging on "index the corpus against a stated capability schema."

## 3. Data composition

> *"We compile a diverse and high-quality set of vision-language data to formulate our **pre-training and mid-training mixtures**. Specifically, the data composition integrates **low-level visual perception data** with **dedicated datasets formulated for embodied tasks and spatial cognition**. To construct the overall training corpus, these specialized, domain-specific data sources are **directly combined with large-scale general understanding data**."*

Four named coverage areas across **>100M samples**: **basic perception · spatial perception · embodied perception · reasoning and planning.**

Plus: *"By constructing **real robot data** and **high-quality reasoning data**, we improve the model's ability to solve real-world problems and complex tasks."*

**Two-phase mixture design:**
| Phase | Content | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Pre-training** | Visual perception data: 2D/3D grounding, depth estimation, segmentation | Physical grounding from the start |
| **Mid-training** | *"blending rich embodied and spatial datasets with general-domain data"* | *"aligns the model's capabilities with downstream embodied requirements"* while preventing general-capability erosion |

The general-domain blend at mid-training is the same anti-forgetting device as Qwen-RobotManip's 28M VL stream and GR-3's VL co-training.

## 4. Self-evolving post-training — data generated by the training loop

> *"we design an **iterative, self-evolving post-training paradigm**. We iteratively improve the thinking abilities of our model by using **a small amount of cold start data**, combined with **iterative reinforcement learning** and **rejection-sampling supervised finetuning (SFT)**."*

Then: *"through a **large-to-small on-policy distillation** approach to transfer knowledge from the large model to the [small one]."*

**Rejection-sampling SFT is a data-curation mechanism**: the model generates candidate reasoning traces, a verifier keeps only the good ones, and those become the next round's training data. Data quality is *produced* by filtering model output rather than by filtering collected data — the same logic as RoboCurate's Best-of-N and LingBot's VLM-scored generation, applied to reasoning rather than to trajectories. The corpus bootstraps itself from a small cold-start seed.

| Model | Role | Data note |

| **Hy-Embodied-VLM-1.0** | Perception, at ~1/10 the compute | — |

Architecture support for the data strategy: **HY-ViT 2.0** with native arbitrary-resolution input, *"a larger scale of pre-training data,"* a tiny LLM providing language supervision signals, and **visual reconstruction supervision to ensure minimal information loss in the visual signals fed to the LLM** — a data-fidelity concern addressed in the encoder rather than the corpus.

## 6. What they do not do
- No numeric filtering thresholds or rejection rates published.
- No dedup stage described.
- Source-level provenance and hour breakdowns are not tabulated.
- No fitted data-scaling curve.

## 7. Transferable takeaways
1. **Write the capability taxonomy first, then build the mixture to fill it.** Coverage becomes auditable and gaps become visible.
2. **Put geometric perception (grounding, depth, segmentation) in the *earliest* pretraining mixture**, not in adaptation — physical grounding is claimed not to be retrofittable.
3. **Blend general-domain data at mid-training** to prevent capability erosion while specializing.
4. **Rejection-sampling SFT manufactures high-quality data** from a small cold-start seed — curation applied to model outputs rather than to collected data.
5. **Add reconstruction supervision to the visual encoder** if you care about information loss between pixels and the LLM — a data-fidelity fix at the architecture level.
